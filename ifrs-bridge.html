What this bridge is, and what it is not
Two populations
IF.Data is the prudential reporting set. The reported IFRS figures come from each bank’s own statements. They answer different questions and are not expected to tie.
Consolidation
Where a bank consolidates an insurance or payments subsidiary, assets arrive in IFRS that IF.Data either excludes or treats differently. That single effect explains the largest gaps here.
Materiality
Gaps below 0.05% are shown but counted as exact ties. A tie is not a validation of either source.
Vintage
IFRS figures are as filed for the reference period. IF.Data may be revised later without a restatement flag.
