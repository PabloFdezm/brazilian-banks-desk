How this dossier is calculated
ROE
Net income ÷ average equity, as reported. Quarterly, not annualised.
Cohort
The universe selected in the top bar. Changing it changes every median and rank on this page.
Funding
Equity and deposits are reported lines; “other liabilities” is the residual of total assets less equity and deposits. A large residual means wholesale funding, repos or insurance float.
Coverage
Provisions ÷ credit portfolio. Read as a stock ratio, not a flow.

Missing quarters are left as gaps, never interpolated. IF.Data revises prior quarters without a restatement flag, so treat early points as vintage data.
